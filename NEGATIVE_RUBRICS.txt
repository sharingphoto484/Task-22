## Nice-to-Have Criteria (Interpretive/Reasoning Errors)

**1. States that the DCT coefficient magnitude of 75.3423 establishes predictable periodic cycles in shipping deviations that will recur in future periods.**
*-15 points · nice to have criteria*
**Rationale:** Treats frequency-domain decomposition as evidence of deterministic recurring patterns rather than a mathematical transformation of historical sample data.

**2. States that the Gamma GLM coefficient of 0.0011 demonstrates that discount changes cause proportional changes in extended value.**
*-12 points · nice to have criteria*
**Rationale:** Confuses observational association with causal mechanism, ignoring confounders and reverse causality.

**3. States that the maximum rolling Herfindahl average of 0.584 represents a permanent equilibrium concentration level in customer ordering patterns.**
*-12 points · nice to have criteria*
**Rationale:** Treats a maximum value from a rolling window as a stable structural feature rather than a peak observation.

**4. States that the MDS stress value of 2.3870 measures the quality or clarity of geographic market segmentation in the customer base.**

**5. States that the standard deviation of 0.165 days proves consistent and uniform fulfillment performance that will persist across all future periods.**
*-10 points · nice to have criteria*

**6. States that the 307 terminal leaves represent distinct product diversity categories or meaningful expenditure groupings in the business.**
*-10 points · nice to have criteria*
**Rationale:** Confuses algorithmic partitioning behavior with substantive product categorization or business segments.

**7. States that the stationary probability of 0.869 predicts that 86.9% of orders will reach this specific status.**
*-12 points · nice to have criteria*

**8. States that the Pearson correlation of 0.0000 proves there is no relationship between customer order frequency and geographic location.**

**9. States that the seasonal demand surges identified in the verdict occur at predictable times with consistent magnitudes that enable reliable capacity planning.**

**10. States that the fitted statistical models identify permanent structural characteristics that will remain stable for long-term strategic planning.**

**11. States that increasing discounts will optimize revenue based on the positive Gamma GLM coefficient of 0.0011.**

**13. States that the DCT analysis reveals the root causes of shipping delays.**

**18. States that the monthly deviation standard deviation of 0.165 establishes a service level agreement benchmark.**
