MODEL FAILURE ANALYSIS FOR WELLBEING PATTERNS ASSESSMENT

The model reported 0.2106 for the mean coefficient of variation across all three years, but the correct value is 0.2114, likely because the model used population standard deviation with n divisor instead of sample standard deviation with n minus 1 divisor when computing the coefficient of variation for each individual year before averaging. This error of 0.0008 in the mean CV suggests systematic underestimation stemming from incorrect degrees of freedom in variance calculations, or the model may have failed to properly filter the dataset to include only the 144 common countries present across all three years during the coefficient of variation computation. The compounding effect of using biased variance estimators across all three years led to an overall mean that underestimates the true relative variability in wellbeing scores throughout the study period, potentially misleading interpretations about temporal trends in Score dispersion.
