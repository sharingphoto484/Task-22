=========================================================================
USAGE GUIDELINES
=========================================================================

These negative rubrics penalize interpretive failures where the model:

1. Confuses correlation with causation
2. Treats in-sample statistics as out-of-sample predictions
3. Interprets sample features as permanent structural properties
4. Infers fundamental relationships from statistical associations
5. Generalizes sample results beyond their valid scope
6. Treats model fit as proof of true data-generating mechanisms
7. Makes causal claims from observational regression results

Each criterion is:
- Atomic (single discrete error)
- Outcome-focused (evaluates what is stated, not how)
- Self-contained (understandable without external context)
- Maximum 20 words in length
- Weighted proportional to severity of interpretive error
- Traceable to common quantitative reasoning failures
