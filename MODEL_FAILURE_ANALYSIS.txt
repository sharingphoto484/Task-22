MODEL FAILURE ANALYSIS

INCORRECT WITHIN R SQUARED VALUE

The model reported 0.0994 for the two way fixed effects within R squared of Happiness Score regressed on the six predictors with country and year effects, but the correct value is 0.1274, likely because the model failed to properly calculate the within transformation by not demeaning both the actual and fitted values using country specific means before computing the ratio of explained to total variance. The within R squared specifically measures the proportion of variance explained after removing country level fixed effects, which requires subtracting each country's mean happiness score and mean fitted value from their respective observations then calculating one minus the ratio of residual to total sum of squares on these demeaned quantities. The incorrect value of 0.0994 suggests the model may have computed a standard R squared, used an improper demeaning procedure such as demeaning only the dependent variable or predictors but not the fitted values, or calculated the between R squared instead of the within R squared which would measure cross sectional rather than temporal variation.
