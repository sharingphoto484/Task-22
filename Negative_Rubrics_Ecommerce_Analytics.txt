## Failure Ideas Repository: Ecommerce Product and Seller Analytics

### Failure Idea 1: K-Means Clusters Represent Natural Product Categories

**Failure Idea:** The analyst concludes that the four k-means clusters represent natural, inherent product categories within the marketplace that reflect true underlying market segments. This reasoning treats an algorithmic partitioning based on Euclidean distance in standardized physical dimension space as evidence of meaningful business categories, when in fact k-means clusters are sensitive to initialization, the choice of k equals four was specified arbitrarily rather than derived from data, and the clustering reflects mathematical similarity in physical dimensions rather than consumer behavior, profitability, or strategic relevance. The clusters are descriptive partitions of the current catalog based on size and weight standardization, not discovered economic segments with independent validity or actionable business meaning.

**Corresponding Rubric Item:**
- *States that k-means clusters represent natural, inherent, or true underlying product categories in the marketplace rather than algorithmic partitions based on physical dimension similarity.*
- *-12 points · nice to have criteria*

### Failure Idea 2: Quantile Regression Coefficient Implies Causal Effect of Length

**Failure Idea:** The analyst interprets the quantile regression coefficient of 97.6268 for product length as indicating that increasing product length causes an increase of approximately 97.63 grams in product weight at the ninetieth percentile, suggesting that sellers should modify product dimensions to achieve target weights. This reasoning confuses conditional association with causal intervention, when in fact the coefficient describes the relationship between length and weight in the existing catalog conditional on height and width, not the effect of manipulating length while holding other physical properties constant. Products are manufactured as integrated physical objects where dimensions and weight are jointly determined by material composition and design, making the coefficient a descriptive summary of current catalog composition rather than a prescription for weight modification through length changes.

**Corresponding Rubric Item:**
- *States that the quantile regression coefficient for product length represents a causal effect or implies that changing product length would cause corresponding changes in product weight.*
- *-15 points · nice to have criteria*

### Failure Idea 3: PCA Variance Explained Indicates Most Important Business Drivers

**Failure Idea:** The analyst concludes that because PC1 explains 46.62 percent of variance in the five features, the variables that load heavily on PC1 are the most important business drivers or strategic priorities for marketplace optimization. This reasoning treats statistical variance decomposition as evidence of business importance, when in fact PCA variance explained measures mathematical redundancy in the standardized feature space, not economic value, revenue impact, customer preferences, or operational significance. A feature could explain substantial variance simply because it has high technical correlation with other measured attributes, while a feature explaining little variance could be the primary driver of conversion rates, customer satisfaction, or profitability that is not captured by the five physical and content attributes included in the analysis.

**Corresponding Rubric Item:**
- *States that variables loading heavily on PC1 or features explaining high variance in PCA represent the most important business drivers or strategic priorities for the marketplace.*
- *-12 points · nice to have criteria*

### Failure Idea 4: Elastic Net Coefficient Selection Proves Causal Relationships

**Failure Idea:** The analyst concludes that because elastic net regression selected product name length with a non-zero coefficient of 50.1237, name length causally influences description length and sellers should be advised to write longer product names to generate more detailed descriptions. This reasoning confuses regularized feature selection with causal identification, when in fact elastic net coefficients reflect predictive associations in the training data under L1 and L2 penalties, not evidence that manipulating name length would cause description length changes. The positive coefficient could arise because certain product categories naturally have both longer names and longer descriptions, or because sellers with more resources invest in both comprehensive titles and detailed content, making the association confounded by unobserved category characteristics or seller quality rather than a direct causal pathway from name to description.

**Corresponding Rubric Item:**
- *States that elastic net coefficient selection or non-zero coefficients prove causal relationships or that manipulating predictor variables would cause changes in the response variable.*
- *-12 points · nice to have criteria*

The clusters describe the current inventory snapshot rather than providing a validated classification system for future catalog expansion.

### Failure Idea 7: Weight-Volume Correlation Enables Predictive Substitution

**Failure Idea:** The analyst concludes that because the Pearson correlation between product weight and volume is 0.8030, volume can reliably substitute for weight in shipping cost calculations or inventory management systems, as the strong correlation proves that knowing volume allows accurate weight prediction. This reasoning confuses correlation strength with prediction accuracy across the full data range, when in fact a correlation of 0.8030 explains only 64.5 percent of variance leaving substantial unexplained variation, the linear relationship may not hold in the tails or for specific product categories, and prediction intervals could be wide enough to cause significant errors in weight-dependent cost estimates. The correlation describes average linear association in the current catalog but does not guarantee that volume-based weight predictions will be sufficiently accurate for operational decisions requiring precise weight information.

**Corresponding Rubric Item:**
- *States that the weight-volume correlation of 0.8030 proves that volume can reliably substitute for weight in operational systems or that correlation strength guarantees accurate prediction.*
- *-10 points · nice to have criteria*

### Failure Idea 8: Poisson Dispersion Near One Validates Model Assumptions

**Failure Idea:** The analyst concludes that because the Poisson dispersion parameter of 1.0729 is close to one, the Poisson regression model assumptions are validated and the model provides trustworthy estimates of how product characteristics causally affect photo count decisions by sellers. This reasoning treats a goodness-of-fit diagnostic as proof of causal validity, when in fact dispersion near one indicates only that the conditional mean and variance are approximately equal in the sample, not that the model captures true causal mechanisms, omitted variables are absent, or the binary predictors heavy product and long description represent the true drivers of seller photography decisions. Low dispersion measures model fit to the count distribution but does not validate the assumption that photo count variation is caused by weight and description length rather than category norms, seller resources, or product visual complexity.

**Corresponding Rubric Item:**
- *States that Poisson dispersion parameter near one validates causal assumptions or proves that the model captures true mechanisms driving the response variable.*
- *-10 points · nice to have criteria*
